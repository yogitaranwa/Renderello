Source and license records remain in the per-source benchmark details, model settings/rights record and the original project archives. This is private review material, not permission to republish third-party source images or branding.

The historical case ID `wine2` is retained for traceability, but its visible source is a blue BAWLS bottle, not a verified wine product. Source IDs describe the saved registry and are not independent product-category verification.
